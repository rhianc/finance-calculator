Monthly compounding (default) M = P × [ r(1+r)n ] / [ (1+r)n − 1 ] Where:
P = loan amount
r = monthly rate (annual ÷ 12)
n = number of months (years × 12)
Continuous compounding M = P × (ert − 1) / (er − 1) ÷ r Or more commonly written as:
M = P × r ert / (ert − 1) Where:
r = continuous annual rate
t = time in years
n = number of monthly payments (t × 12)

Simple calculator for planning. Not financial advice. Most consumer loans use monthly compounding.
