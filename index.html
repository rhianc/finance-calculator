Monthly compounding (default)
$$M = P \times \frac{r(1+r)^{n}}{(1+r)^{n} - 1}$$ Where:
$P$ = loan amount
$r$ = monthly rate (annual ÷ 12)
$n$ = number of months (years × 12)
Continuous compounding
$$M = P \times \frac{r e^{rt}}{e^{rt} - 1}$$ Where:
$r$ = continuous annual rate
$t$ = time in years
$n$ = number of monthly payments ($t \times 12$)

Simple calculator for planning. Not financial advice. Most consumer loans use monthly compounding.
