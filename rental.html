How the comparison works

Once a month. The mortgage rate is divided by 12. Appreciation, rent growth, and the stock return are effective annual rates. Figures on the chart are after inflation.

Loan

\(P\) is the amount borrowed, \(r\) the monthly rate, \(N\) the number of payments.

\[ M = P \dfrac{r(1+r)^{N}}{(1+r)^{N}-1} \]

Cash flow

Collected rent is the asking rent times \(1-v\), where \(v\) is vacancy. The asking rent starts from the local gross yield and moves with price to the power of 0.85. Management is a share of collected rent. Depreciation is the building basis divided by 27.5 years. It does not leave the bank, but it does change the tax.

\[ \mathrm{CF} = \text{collected} - \text{operating costs} - M - \text{income tax} \]

Income tax is federal, state, and the 3.8% net investment income tax on profit. A loss offsets wages only up to the $25,000 allowance, which phases out from $100,000 to $150,000 of income. The rest waits until you sell. There is no primary-residence exclusion.

The two accounts

\(S\) starts as the down payment plus closing costs. Negative cash flow is added to \(S\). Positive cash flow is added to \(T\), invested beside the house. Both earn the stock return after drag.

\[ S_t = S_{t-1}(1+r_s) + \max(0,-\mathrm{CF}_t) \]
\[ T_t = T_{t-1}(1+r_s) + \max(0,\mathrm{CF}_t) \]

If you sold that month

\[ \mathrm{Gain} = \max\left(0,\ H(1-c_s) - (\mathrm{basis} - \mathrm{depreciation})\right) \]

Depreciation taken so far is taxed at your ordinary rate, up to 25%. The rest of the gain is long-term after a year. Suspended losses offset that gain first. What you keep is the sale price after selling costs, minus the loan and the tax, plus \(T\).

Left out on purpose

  • 1031 exchanges, cost segregation, and bonus depreciation.
  • The mid-month depreciation convention, and improvements beyond the maintenance rate.
  • Real estate professional status, and the qualified business income deduction.
  • Refinancing, extra principal, and adjustable rates.
  • Rent control, and returns that don't arrive in a straight line.

For a rental, not a home you live in. Brackets are tax year 2026. Depreciation is 27.5 years. The loss allowance is the federal active-participation limit. Appreciation is FHFA, 1991–2026.
